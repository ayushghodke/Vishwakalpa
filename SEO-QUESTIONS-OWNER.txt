 A NOTE ON WHY WE'RE ASKING SO MUCH
-------------------------------------------------------------------------------
 The SEO plan tells us which searches to target. It doesn't tell us what
 Vishwakalpa actually does, or what's true.

 We cannot write pages claiming services you don't offer. Google has become
 very good at spotting this, and more importantly, it would bring you enquiries
 you don't want and can't serve — which wastes your team's time and actually
 makes your Google ranking worse, because people click and immediately leave.

 The single most valuable thing you can give us is real detail about real
 projects. One page describing an actual factory you designed — with the
 client, the city, the size, and a photo — will outperform ten pages of
 general writing about factory design. Google rewards specificity and so do
 the people reading it.
